The standard error is NA for (Intercept), z3, x_c: every observation that identifies those coefficients is fitted exactly, so the variance is built from residuals that are all numerically zero. Reported as a number it would be zero or rounding error, and its t statistic would put p at 0.
Warning in lm_robust_fit(y = outcome, X = X, weights = weights, cluster = cluster,  :
  3 observations have a computed leverage at or near 1, which happens when the design is close to saturated and the observation is fitted exactly or nearly so. `se_type = "HC2"` divides by (1 - leverage), so they are dropped from the variance. The standard error is NA for (Intercept), z2, z3, x_c, z2:x_c, whose coefficient those observations alone identify: dropping them leaves nothing to estimate that variance from. Every other standard error in this fit is the one the design without them gives. Use `se_type = "HC1"` or `"classical"`, or drop covariates, to use every observation.
Some coefficients are collinear with other regressors and were dropped, and are returned as NA: z3:x_c. A dropped treatment interaction is not a dropped covariate: with x_c no longer interacted with the treatment, z3 is no longer the effect at the covariate means, since its slope is now constrained equal across treatment arms. Where the dependency runs through the treatment column itself, the reported value also depends on which column was dropped, and the effect at the covariate means is not identified.
